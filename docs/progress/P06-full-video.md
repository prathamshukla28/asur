# P06 — The Full Video  ✅ DONE

## What this phase is for

Once the hero proof is approved, make the whole video — carefully, section by
section, so a weak part can be re-made on its own without redoing everything.

## What P06 added

- **Section-by-section QA**: each section is checked on its rendered output; only
  the sections that fail get re-made — the good ones are never touched. A section
  with no rendered video does not pass.
- A **5-family final check**: picture, sound, text/typography, content accuracy,
  and platform fit (size, length, safe areas).
- From your "ideas 3" brief: the **Human Pass Test** — a human has to confirm, on
  the real rendered video, that it looks like genuine live-action (natural motion,
  real skin, steady lighting, no warping/glitching hands/floating objects, proper
  color grading). ASUR won't claim this for you; if you haven't reviewed it, the
  check waits (HOLD) rather than passing.
- Also from "ideas 3": a **duration target band** (e.g. 30–40s) — if the video is
  outside it, you get a warning, not a block.

## Safety promises kept

- The judgement is always on the **actual rendered video**, not on the plan.
- A required human check that hasn't been done **holds**; it is never auto-passed.
