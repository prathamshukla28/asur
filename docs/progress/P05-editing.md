# P05 — The Editing Room  ✅ DONE

## What this phase is for

A real editing environment where you stay in control. ASUR assists; it never takes
over.

## What P05 added

- A **timeline** (industry-standard OTIO format) at 1080×1920 vertical, where every
  clip keeps its full version history.
- The **15 motion effects** (text reveal, word punch, camera push/pull, cut-on-beat,
  highlight word, parallax, speed ramp, etc.) — used as tools, never as the creative
  driver.
- Caption styling, including proper Hindi/Marathi (Devanagari) shaping, with safe-area
  checks so text never sits in the top/bottom danger zones.
- Audio mixing that refuses to let the music bury your voice.
- Full manual control: accept, reject, modify, regenerate, lock, compare, branch,
  revert — every change saved as a new version you can revert, and nothing is ever
  silently overwritten.
