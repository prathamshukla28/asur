# P07 — The Honest Viral Check  ✅ DONE

## What this phase is for

The judge. It asks "does this video have a real chance of performing well?" — and
answers **honestly**, never with a single fake "viral %".

## What P07 added

- **12 separate scores** (0–100 each): Attention, Retention, Satisfaction, Value,
  Shareability, Saveability, Replayability, Originality, Authenticity, Visual
  Quality, Audience Fit, Platform Fit — always shown individually.
- **4 evidence classes** on every claim: Verified Fact / Strong Evidence /
  Empirical Observation (from your own past results) / Hypothesis.
- **Binary risk flags** (yes/no warnings), not fake percentage penalties.
- A verdict: **PASS / PASS-WITH-CHANGES / REJECT**.

## Safety promises kept

- Never a single "%viral" number (there is even a test that checks for this).
- The judge only ever sees its own view (the quality targets are stripped out by name).
